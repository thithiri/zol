Zcash Operating Layer

Programmable Zcash privacy on Solana SVM.

ZOL lets any Solana program take shielded ZEC, run real-time sessions in a MagicBlock/SVM rollup, and pay the result back to Zcash. Apps get the speed and composability of the SVM; players keep Zcash privacy: no SOL, no Solana wallet, and nothing public that links their play to their Zcash wallet.

How it works

Deposit, play, withdraw. The ZEC never leaves Zcash.

It sits in the app's shielded vault while a ledger on Solana records who is owed what. Play happens in a MagicBlock/SVM rollup, at rollup speed.

Guarantees

What ZOL guarantees, and who enforces it.

No wrapped ZEC. ZEC stays in a shielded vault on Zcash; balances are ledger entries, not tokens. Nothing to bridge, nothing to depeg.

No value created. A pot returns exactly what went in; balances only move between a player, pots and exits.

Payouts go where the player said. The ledger fixes the payout address at the first deposit, as a commitment, and each operator checks a payout against it before signing.

Nobody is trapped. A pot that never comes back from the rollup is refunded after its session deadline plus 65 minutes, and a frozen app still returns pots and accepts exit requests.

Apps stay in their lane. An app can't add or swap a player, touch another app's money, or change a payout address.

Play and wallet stay apart in public. Solana sees session keys and commitments; Zcash sees shielded notes. Nothing public links the two; the operators can.

What you still trust

  • The operators (2 of 3) to stay live, sign only true credits, and not collude: a quorum holds the vault's spend key. Each honest operator pays only what the ledger settled, to the committed address. They also see which session key belongs to which Zcash address.
  • The rollup validator for outcomes while a pot is in the rollup. It can reorder or withhold; withholding turns into a refund.
  • The app's own logic for who wins. The ledger checks that seats add up, not that the right seat won.

For developers

Your app is a Solana program with Zcash privacy.

Write it in Anchor, like any Solana program. ZOL handles the money: shielded deposits, balances, exits and payouts. You write the rules: open a pot, seat players, let the rollup decide, and the ledger pays the seats when the pot comes home.

  • Quickstart: a player key, a shielded deposit, coin flips and a withdrawal, from code, in about 15 minutes.
  • Build an app: the anatomy of a ZOL app, walked through on the coin flip.
  • Security model: who can do what, and a review checklist for your app.
  • Deploying: on the testnet, send the operators your program id and source; they register, activate and cap it.
