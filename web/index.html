        How it works, and what you trust
        • Deposit shielded ZEC from your Zcash wallet: it goes to the app's vault, held by three operators (2 of 3 sign everything), and a Solana ledger keeps your balance.
        • Play any of the games with that balance. Each runs in a MagicBlock rollup; the money only moves as the ledger allows.
        • Withdraw to the address you gave at your first deposit: the ledger fixes it, and the operators can't send it elsewhere.
