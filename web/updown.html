      Your rounds and their proof
        How it works, and what you trust
        • Start a table: you and the house each put in up to 10 stakes, and the table moves into a MagicBlock rollup once. Each bet is one stake (0.001 TAZ) on ZEC/USD going up or down over the next 30 s.
        • The price is Pyth's, live in the rollup. A bet settles at the first price published after its 30 s: a right call pays 1.9× (the house's seat pays 0.9 stake), a wrong one pays its stake to the house, an unchanged price refunds it. Not settled within 10 s of expiry: void, nothing moves.
        • The table returns to Solana when you stop, run out, or leave it idle; the payout to your balance happens as it returns.
