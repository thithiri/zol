Flip a coin against the house

Shielded ZEC in, a fair coin, shielded ZEC out. Zcash testnet, Solana devnet and a MagicBlock rollup; no real money. You only need a Zcash testnet wallet, or not even that: use “Fund me”.

How it works, and what you trust
  • Your 0.01 TAZ goes to the app's Zcash vault, which three operators hold together (2 of 3 must sign anything).
  • A ledger on Solana records your balance. The house is a second player with its own balance: it matches your stake, nothing more.
  • The coin is flipped in a MagicBlock rollup with a verifiable random function: neither you nor the house picks the outcome. You trust the rollup validator for the result.
  • When the game ends, your winnings are paid out automatically to the Zcash address you give, from the vault. The operators can't pay anyone else: the ledger fixes where it goes.
