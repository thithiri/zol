        How it works, and what you trust
        • Your ZEC goes to the app's shielded Zcash vault, held by three operators (2 of 3 sign everything). A Solana ledger keeps your game balance.
        • The house is a second player with its own balance: it matches your stake. The coin is decided by a verifiable random function in a MagicBlock rollup: neither you nor the house can pick it. Heads or tails is your call; who wins is the VRF's.
        • Cash-outs go only to the address you gave at your first deposit: the ledger fixes it, and the operators can't send it elsewhere.
